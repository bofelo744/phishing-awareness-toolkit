what is phishing?
phishing is a communication that seems to be legit. A cyber criminal can pretend to be from a legit company and trick someone into giving them personal information
TYPES OF PHISHING
Vishing; using of phone calls to trick someone into revealing information
spear phishing; targeted attackers on specific individual or job roles
email phishing; fake email that trick someone into clicking it. it may state that the account is locked
HOW TO SPOT A FAKE EMAIL 
Urgent language for example, click the verification link to keep using the email
asking for password because real IT will never ask for a password
using the wrong email format
WHAT TO DO WHEN SEEING PHISHING
STOP- do not click
THINK- does this exist
VERIFY- call the upper department or IT 
REPORT- call authorised people 
REMEMBER: STOP. VERIFY.CLICK
